Project Summary

In today's complex digital landscape, ensuring compliance with privacy regulations like GDPR is crucial yet challenging. The **GDPR Scanner** is a lightweight, AI-powered web tool designed to simplify this process.

It automates the assessment of your privacy policies, consent mechanisms, and cookie behaviors, checking for compliance with the EU GDPR, Google Consent Mode v2, and with future alignment planned for PIPEDA/Law 25 (Canada) and UK GDPR.

Our tool is built specifically for **startups, web developers, privacy consultants, and small eCommerce platforms** that need a fast, transparent, and affordable solution for GDPR self-auditing, without the need for expensive legal teams.

How It Works: Core Functionalities

1. GDPR Article Compliance Checker

Upload your privacy policy text, and our scanner goes to work:

  • Parses policy content to identify key GDPR elements.
  • Detects compliance with critical articles:
    • Article 5 – Principles of processing
    • Article 6 – Lawfulness of processing
    • Article 7 – Conditions for consent
    • Article 13 – Information & transparency
    • Article 30 – Records of processing activities
  • Uses a robust section-wise keyword and regex logic.

Scoring Labels: ✅ Compliant, ⚠ Potentially Compliant, ❌ Requires Review

Output Formats: JSON, Markdown, HTML (PDF export coming soon!)

2. Consent Mode v2 Integration WIP

Align your analytics and advertising with user consent effectively:

  • Tracks Google’s Consent Mode v2 signals: ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization.
  • Integrates seamlessly with Google Tag Manager (GTM) and popular Cookie CMPs like Cookiebot and Funding Choices.
  • Scans for pre-consent cookie behavior to flag potential violations.

3. GDPR Gap Assessment Report Planned

Gain immediate insights into your compliance posture:

  • Color-coded compliance meter (Green/Yellow/Red) for each GDPR article.
  • Quantified scores, e.g., “4/6 principles met under Article 5.”
  • Exportable as PDF, HTML, and Markdown for easy sharing and record-keeping.

4. Cookie & Tracker Scanner Upcoming

Ensure your website respects user privacy from the ground up:

  • Uses regex logic to detect common tracking methods like document.cookie.
  • Identifies Google and Facebook trackers.
  • Flags scripts that load cookies before explicit user consent, aligning with ePrivacy and GDPR requirements.
