Sample Privacy Policy - FinCloud Analytics
Effective Date: 15 March 2025
Company: FinCloud Analytics GmbH
Website: www.fincloud.ai
Contact: privacy@fincloud.ai | DPO: dpo@fincloud.ai
1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines how we process, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the GDPR and applicable data protection laws.
2. Data Categories Collected
We collect personal data such as full name, email address, device identifiers, geolocation, browsing history, behavioral data, and financial account activity from users across our platforms.
3. Lawful Basis
We rely on user consent (Article 6(1)(a)), contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b)), and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for processing. We maintain records of each lawful basis used per processing activity.
4. Special Categories
We process special categories of data (e.g., financial health indicators, biometric data) only with explicit user consent as per Article 9.
5. Children's Data
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. Age verification is implemented during onboarding.
6. Rights of Data Subjects
We enable access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability via the User Dashboard or by email request.
7. Profiling and Automated Decisions
User profiles are created for credit scoring, with human review to mitigate fully automated decisions.
8. Data Sharing
We share personal data with affiliated processors in the EU, US (via SCCs), and Japan (adequacy status). Data Sharing Agreements are in place.
9. Data Transfers
Cross-border Transfers are conducted under SCCs and Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs).
10. Security Measures
FinCloud applies ISO/IEC 27001 controls, pseudonymization, continuous monitoring, and employee training.
11. Data Retention
Data is stored only as long as needed for the original processing purpose, with automatic deletion protocols.
12. Data Breaches
Data breaches are logged and reported to authorities and users per Articles 33 and 34.
13. DPIAs
We conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for any new AI-driven service or feature.
